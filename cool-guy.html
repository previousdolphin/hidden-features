A brief, mostly true biography

Origins

Cool Guy was allegedly a beta tester for the original Snake build. Legend says he found the hidden trigger in fourteen seconds and has been insufferable about it ever since.

Job description

Officially: "Vault Integrity Consultant." Unofficially: he leans against the Pong cabinet and decides who's earned the right to unlock Parcheesi.

Known weaknesses

High-fives, dramatic lighting, and any UI element that makes a satisfying click sound. See below for a live demonstration.
